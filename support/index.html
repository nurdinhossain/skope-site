Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the You tab, tap Delete account, and confirm twice. This permanently deletes your profile, goals, steps, photos, conversations and notes. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

How do I log out, and how do I get back in?

Open the You tab and tap Log out. To come back, tap Continue with Apple on the sign-in screen using the same Apple ID, and your goals will be there.

Why wasn't my proof photo accepted?

Skope compares your photo with the "what done looks like" description for that step. If it clearly doesn't match, the step is marked failed and you can submit another photo while the goal is still open. If the photo is unclear, the step goes to "In review" and a person decides. If you think a result is wrong, email us.

I'm not getting check-in notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings, then Notifications, then Skope. Your daily check-in arrives at the time you chose during setup, and only when you have an active goal. Opening the app after that time also brings up your check-in.

It says I've reached today's limit.

To keep the service fair and sustainable, each account has a daily limit on AI requests. It resets the next day (UTC). If you keep hitting it with normal use, let us know.
