Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

This policy explains what Skope, an app operated by Nurdin Hossain ("Skope", "we", "us"), collects when you use the Skope iPhone app, what we do with it, who else handles it, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • You sign in with Apple. We don't ask for your email address, and we have no passwords.
  • We store the goals, steps, photos and conversations you create so the app can work.
  • An AI provider (Anthropic) reads your goal text, messages and proof photos to write replies, plan steps and check proof.
  • We use PostHog to count which features are used. It never receives the text of your goals or messages, or your photos.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

InformationWhat it is
Account An identifier from Sign in with Apple. We ask Apple only to confirm who you are. Apple may share your name with the app when you first sign in; we don't store it. We do not request your email address.
Profile The name you type during setup, the goal categories you choose, your coaching style and the obstacles you picked in setup, your answers to the short survey about how you pursue goals (past goals and resolutions, how you plan, track and start them), and the Commitment score worked out from them, which then follows what you do in the app and is kept as a daily history for the graph on your profile. These shape how your coach talks to you. We also record when you finished setup.
Goals and progress Goal titles and categories, deadlines, the steps (milestones) and their descriptions and due dates, their status, how we rated each goal when you created it (whether it was specific, measurable, challenging and doable, and its difficulty), the points you earn for finishing goals, and any small "side quests" you keep.
Photos Only photos you choose to submit as proof for a step or a side quest. The app re-saves each photo before uploading, which removes embedded details such as location. Photos are stored privately and are only available to your account.
Conversations Your messages with your character (casual chats and daily check-ins), your chosen mood, the summaries and ratings generated from check-ins, and short notes the character keeps so it can remember what matters to you.
Reminders The time of day you chose for your daily check-in. The reminder notifications are scheduled on your own phone by the app; we don't send them from our servers.
Usage Which steps and screens you reach and actions such as creating a goal, submitting a proof photo or finishing a check-in, tied to your account identifier. For a goal we record only its category (or "custom"), whether it repeats, and whether it has a time, never what you wrote.
Technical A count of how many AI requests your account has made each day (to prevent abuse), and standard server logs. Your sign-in session is kept in your iPhone's secure Keychain.

How we use it

  • To run the app: create your goals and steps, show your progress, and remember your account.
  • To power the AI features: review whether a goal is clear and reachable, suggest steps for a goal, reply as your character, write check-in summaries, and compare a proof photo with what you said "done" looks like.
  • To keep the service safe and fair, including limiting how much AI each account can use per day.
  • To answer your questions and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

If the AI can't tell whether a photo shows what you described, a person at Skope may look at that photo to decide. We use your information only for these purposes.

Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers to operate Skope. They process data on our behalf.

  • Supabase hosts our database and stores your photos. Our project runs in Canada. Each account can only read its own rows, and files are kept in a private folder per user.
  • Anthropic provides the AI. When you use an AI feature, the relevant goal text, step descriptions, messages, character notes and any proof photo you submit are sent to Anthropic to produce a response. Anthropic's published policy says it does not use API inputs and outputs to train its models, and that it keeps them for a limited time (up to 30 days under its current policy), except where content is flagged for violating its usage policy (kept longer) or the law requires otherwise. See Anthropic's data retention page.
  • PostHog provides product analytics, so we can see which parts of the app are used and fix what isn't working. It receives the usage events above, with your account identifier, plus basic device details (app version, iOS version, device model). It does not receive your goals, messages or photos.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and delivers the notifications scheduled on your phone.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and we do not use advertising software. The only analytics we use is PostHog, as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or security of our users or the service.

Where it's processed

Your account data is stored in Canada. AI requests are processed by Anthropic, which operates in the United States and other countries. Usage events go to PostHog's servers in the United States. By using Skope you understand your information may be processed outside the country where you live.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, goals, steps, photos, conversations and notes from our database and file storage, and we ask Apple to end the Sign in with Apple connection for Skope. Any backups our hosting provider keeps expire on their normal schedule, and Anthropic keeps requests for the period described above. Usage events stay in PostHog under your account identifier after deletion; email us and we will delete them too. Server logs are kept for a short time.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: open the You tab, tap Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your data.
  • Access or correct your information, or ask a question: email us at the address below.
  • Notifications: turn them off in iOS Settings, then Notifications, then Skope.
  • Photos: you decide which photos to submit; the app never reads your photo library on its own.

Residents of some US states, including California, have rights to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We honor these requests for everyone who uses Skope. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Children

Skope is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, accounts can only read their own records, and photos are stored privately. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong passcode on your phone.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Skope handles information, we will update this page and its date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
